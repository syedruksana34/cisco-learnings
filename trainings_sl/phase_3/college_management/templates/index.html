Supported file formats

PDF; Word DOC/DOCX; Excel XLS/XLSX; PowerPoint PPT/PPTX; OpenDocument ODT/ODS/ODP; text, CSV, Markdown, JSON, XML, HTML, logs and common source-code files; PNG, JPG, TIFF, BMP, WebP; MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC, AAC; MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, AVI, MPEG.

Images and scanned PDF pages use English OCR. Audio/video uses local speech transcription, not visual scene understanding. Archives, executables, encrypted and unknown formats are rejected.

One file, max 5 MB / 50,000 extracted characters. PDFs: 100 pages, at most 20 OCR pages. Recordings: 10 minutes. Local processing may take up to 3 minutes. Original files are not retained.
